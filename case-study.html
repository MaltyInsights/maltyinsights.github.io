Document collection · Case study

309 scattered documents, collected into one checked file

The client had years of attachments and shared links buried in email threads. The job was to collect them, remove duplicates and make it clear what had and had not been found.

The work

The problem

Several years of documents existed only as attachments and shared links inside email threads. Nobody could say how many there were. Two earlier attempts had produced folders that looked complete, but documents were still missing when someone went looking for them.

Collecting the files was only half the job. The client also needed to know how many had been found and where each one came from.

What I did

  1. I checked every thread for links and attachments, including files buried in quoted replies.
  2. Each document was stored with its thread and date, so the client could find the source again.
  3. Duplicates were matched by their contents rather than their filenames. The same document had been sent under four different names.
  4. I counted the words at the source and again in the final file, then compared the two counts.

Missing files were named

If a source file could no longer be retrieved, it was listed with the email thread it came from. The client could follow it up instead of assuming the final folder was complete.

Time spent

The processing took less than a day of machine time and just over half an hour of the client's time for the brief and final check.
