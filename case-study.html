Document collection · What the client got

309 scattered documents, collected into one checked file

The client had years of attachments and shared links buried in email threads. I collected them, removed the duplicates, and handed back one file with a clear note of what could and could not be found.

309
documents found, repeats removed, delivered as one file

The receipt, in plain terms

What I checked. Every email thread for links and attachments, including files buried in quoted replies, matched by their contents rather than their filenames.

What I found. 309 documents once duplicates were removed. The same document had sometimes been sent under four different names.

What I did not check. A handful of links pointed at documents that no longer existed anywhere. I named those in the delivery instead of leaving them out silently.
