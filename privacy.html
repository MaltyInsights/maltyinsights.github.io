Privacy

A short page, because there isn't much to say: I don't run trackers or visitor-measurement scripts, and I don't keep what you type into the tools on this site.

What I collect

This site collects nothing about you beyond what you send me yourself, by email. If you write to me, I have your email address and whatever you put in the message. That is it. There is no contact form, no account, and no data collected in the background as you browse.

The website checker

The free site check lets you paste in a URL and get back a check of that site. The URL you enter is checked and not kept: it is used to run the check itself, and nothing about it is stored afterwards.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies. It runs no visitor-measurement script, no advertising pixels and no tracking of any kind. Nothing on this site phones a third party. Everything you load here comes from nordverify.com itself.
