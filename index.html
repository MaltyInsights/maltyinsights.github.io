What I do not take on

Three kinds of work I turn down

Saying this before you ask is cheaper for you and for me than finding out halfway through.

Taking payments
Card payments and checkout systems need someone who does only that. I will happily point you at one.
Anything where a break is a safety problem
If the thing failing quietly would hurt someone or lock them out, it needs a specialist, not me.
Personal or health records
Anything covered by medical or similar rules belongs with someone who carries that responsibility properly.

Everything else, tell me and I will say yes or no the same day.
