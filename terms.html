Terms

How I work, in plain terms: what I agree to before I start, what's included, and what I don't take on.

Price

I agree a fixed price with you before I start work. You know the number before anything is built, not after.

Changes

One round of changes is included once I deliver the first version. Anything beyond that round is scoped and priced separately, agreed with you before I do it.

What I don't take on

I don't build payment integrations, I don't take on security-critical work, and I don't handle personal or health data. If a project needs any of those, I'll say so up front rather than taking it on and hoping.

Evidence

Work comes with evidence of what was tested. You do not have to take my word for it. You get to see what was checked and how.
